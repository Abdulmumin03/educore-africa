Empty, loading, error & toast

Every table, chart and panel in the console resolves to one of these four. They share the panel frame, the 8px radius and the same type ramp as the live states, so a screen does not change shape while it loads.

01 · Empty
Centred in the content area the data would have filled — not in a smaller box, so the page does not resize when rows arrive. The subtext says why it is empty, because “no results” and “nothing exists yet” need different actions from the reader.

03 · Error
The panel keeps its frame and gains a red border, so the failure lands where the data would have been rather than as a page-level banner. The technical line is shown, not hidden: an operator reporting this needs something to paste, and the readable sentence above it is what tells them whether to retry or escalate.

04 · Toast
Bottom-right, stacking upward, newest at the bottom. Auto-dismiss after 4s — except where an Undo is offered, which holds for 8s so the window to change your mind is longer than the window to read it.

When each is used
Green — the thing you asked for happened. Add Undo only when the action is genuinely reversible in one call.
Amber — it happened, but not entirely. A broadcast that reached the in-app inbox and not SMS is amber, never green.
Red — nothing happened. Carries the reason, no timer, and stays until dismissed.
Not a toast
Anything the operator must read before continuing — a destructive confirmation, an erasure, a broadcast about to reach 142 schools — is a dialog, not a toast. A message that auto-dismisses is a message you can miss.
